## Description

**EmotionCreator** lets you express what words alone can't.

Create beautifully designed digital message pages for the moments that matter: love, apologies, birthdays, gratitude, missing someone, and more.

**How it works:**
1. Pick an emotion template
2. Write your personal message
3. Share the unique link — no app needed

**Why EmotionCreator?**
- 9 handcrafted templates, each with its own visual identity
- AI-assisted message generation via Claude
- Zero friction — no signup required to create
- Private by design — link-based access only
- Free forever for core features

Perfect for long-distance relationships, heartfelt apologies, surprise birthdays, and any moment when a text message isn't enough.

## FAQ Answers (for PH comments)

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card, no trial. Premium ($2.99 one-time) removes branding from your shared page.

**Q: How long do shared links last?**
A: Forever, until you delete them. If you sign in, you can manage all your messages from a dashboard.

**Q: Can I use this for business?**
A: Absolutely. Many use it for customer thank-you messages, team birthday cards, and appreciation notes.

**Q: What's next on the roadmap?**
A: Video messages, custom domains for premium users, and team workspaces.
